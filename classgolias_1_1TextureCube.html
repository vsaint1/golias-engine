Detailed Description

A cubemap texture resource. Supports loading from HDR/LDR cross-layout images.

Cross-layout images have 6 faces arranged in a cross pattern. Supports both horizontal and vertical cross layouts, and HDR (.hdr) or LDR (PNG, JPG, etc.) formats.

Cube face order: +X, -X, +Y, -Y, +Z, -Z
