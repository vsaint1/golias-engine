Detailed Description

Caches Meshes, Textures, Shaders, and Materials by path.

All assets are shared_ptr-managed — they stay alive as long as any client holds a reference.

Typical usage: auto mesh = assetManager.LoadMesh("res/models/suzanne/suzanne.gltf"); auto tex = assetManager.LoadTexture("res/textures/stone_albedo.png");
