Detailed Description

A container of 3D entities, systems, and behaviors.

Entity management: auto e = scene.CreateEntity("Player"); e.AddComponent<TransformComponent>();

System management (shared logic over many entities): scene.AddSystem<EnemyAISystem>(); scene.GetSystem<EnemyAISystem>()->SetDifficulty(2);

Per-entity scripting: scene.AddBehavior<FreeFlyCamera>(cameraEntity, speed);
