Detailed Description

3D CameraComponent with perspective/orthographic projection.

Usage: CameraComponent cam; cam.SetPerspective(60.0f, 16.0f/9.0f, 0.1f, 1000.0f); Matrix4x4 vp = cam.GetViewProjectionMatrix(transform);
