Detailed Description

Assimp IOSystem that reads files through the engine FileSystem (pack first, then disk). Paths are treated as pack-relative (e.g. "models/foo.gltf") and prefixed with "res://". Assimp takes ownership of this object and deletes it when the Importer is destroyed.
