Detailed Description

Wraps Bullet3D dynamics world for 3D physics simulation.

Manages body creation/destruction and provides collision callbacks. Sync modes:

  • Physics → Transform: after simulation, copy RB positions to TransformComponent
  • Transform → Physics: before simulation, copy TransformComponent to RB
