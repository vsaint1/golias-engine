Detailed Description

Compiles GLSL 450 to SPIR-V and cross-compiles to all target formats.

Thread-safe after Initialize() is called. Uses glslang for GLSL→SPIR-V, spirv-cross for SPIR-V→target, and DXC for HLSL→DXIL (Windows only).
